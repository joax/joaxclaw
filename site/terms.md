## What the hosted web app is

The hosted web app is a convenience: a copy of the open-source web build, served for you,
gated behind an active monthly sponsorship. It is **not** required to use JoaxClaw. The
desktop app is free, and the same web build can be self-hosted for free — instructions are

The app runs entirely in your browser and talks directly to **your own** OpenClaw gateway.
We do not operate a gateway, do not proxy your traffic, and cannot see your conversations,
credentials, or data. What the app can do depends on your gateway, not on us.
